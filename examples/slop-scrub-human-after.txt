This week we honored volunteers at our Washington and College Park locations who each gave more than 100 hours to the National Archives this year. The 295 volunteers contributed 42,284 hours in all. We’re grateful for their love of history and for the time they gave to our work.

A parade of staff supervisors took the stage to brag about their volunteers’ work. The volunteers wrote hundreds of item-level descriptions, created thousands of photo captions, scanned tens of thousands of files, and indexed tens of thousands of records. They also inventoried rows of stacks, answered researchers’ questions, and improved access to our online holdings. They used social media to share information about our records.

Some volunteers wrote articles for our Prologue magazine and blog posts about the records, and some presented lectures to the public. We appreciate how our volunteers help people understand our records and help us serve the people who use them.
